ABOUT ME
================================================================

I'm Roy Matheri Waweru, but most people call me Weru. I'm a
full-stack developer and agentic AI engineer in Nairobi, Kenya,
finishing a BSc in ICT at KCA University.


WHAT I LIKE BUILDING
--------------------
Software that does real work on its own and knows when to stop
and ask first.

  - GigaClaw hunts remote jobs, tailors my CV to each one and
    applies, but waits for my approval before anything is sent.

  - KontentPyper watches X and Reddit for what's trending,
    writes the posts, cuts the video and publishes it.

  - M-Afya lets a patient text a blood pressure or blood sugar
    reading on WhatsApp and get a plain-language answer back.

Most of it runs on Python, FastAPI and LangGraph. The front ends
are Next.js and TypeScript.


BUILT FOR WHERE I LIVE
----------------------
In Kenya the internet drops and customers pay with M-Pesa. So my
POS, MoniePal, keeps selling offline and syncs later, takes
payments through M-Pesa Daraja, and integrates KRA eTIMS for tax
compliance. Those constraints shape the design from the start,
not as an afterthought.


HOW I WORK
----------
I build bottom-up and test each piece on its own before wiring
the pieces together. I deploy on Linux servers I set up myself
(Nginx, systemd, Certbot), and I start on SQLite while keeping
the path to PostgreSQL open.

I live in the terminal. I even built fmitt, a daily newsletter
about Linux and terminal tricks.


WHERE I CAME FROM
-----------------
In 2023 I did a software engineering internship at Yashio Kenya,
fixing bugs in a TypeScript codebase next to more experienced
